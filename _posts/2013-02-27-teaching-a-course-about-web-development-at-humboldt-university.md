Hello everyone, just a quick note but I'm extremely excited to finally announce that I will be teaching a course about the basics of web development at Humboldt University Berlin. The course is meant for bachelor students who don't study computer science. It's a total basics course, no prior knowledge required. The course will start with basic HTML and CSS, then we'll get into some Ruby and then some Ruby on Rails. So in short, it could be perfect to be the start of your journey into Programming. And a useful skill to have, in [the spirit of this awesome video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nKIu9yen5nc&feature=player_embedded). The course will be every week on Wednesday for 9 weeks (starting in May) for ~5 hours + breaks. The course will be held in German. The course is limited to ~14 students. **[Link to the full course description (German)](http://www.careercenter.hu-berlin.de/kurse/sommersemester/sose13/bachelorkurse/6059)** If you are a student interested in the course, feel free to get in touch with me for questions etc! In the end, a **big thanks to everyone who helped making this happen!** Especially [Dajana](https://twitter.com/DG_Tweety) :-) Cheers, Tobi
