This is the first talk I ever gave about my two new favorite technologies to play with (at home and at work) - [Elixir](http://elixir-lang.org/) and [Phoenix](http://www.phoenixframework.org/). I gave this talk at [Vilnius.rb in march](http://www.meetup.com/vilniusrb/events/223797393/) and at the [Ruby User Group Berlin in Apri](http://www.rug-b.de/events/april-meetup-2016)l. Hope you enjoy it.

#### Abstract

> Elixir and Phoenix are all the hype lately - what's great about them? Is there more to them than "just" fast, concurrent and reliable? This talk will give a short intro into both Elixir and Phoenix, highlighting strengths, differences from Ruby/Rails and weaknesses.
