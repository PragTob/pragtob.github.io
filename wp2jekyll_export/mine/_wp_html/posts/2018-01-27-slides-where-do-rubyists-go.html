I gave my first ever keynote yesterday at Ruby on Ice, which was a lot of fun. A lot of the talk is based on my "Where do Rubyists go?"-survey but also researching and looking into languages. The talk looks into what programming languages Ruby developers learn for work or in their free time, what the major features of those languages are and how that compares to Ruby. What does it tell us about Ruby and our community? Slides can be viewed here or on speakerdeck, slideshare or PDF [slideshare id=86779279&doc=rubyonice-180127101329]

Abstract

Many Rubyists branch out and take a look at other languages. What are similarities between those languages and ruby? What are differences? How does Ruby influence these languages?
